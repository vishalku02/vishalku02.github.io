I am a first-year graduate student at Stanford University, where I'm advised by Dora Demszky. I'm interested in building collaborative AI systems that build and augment human expertise. I'm particularly curious about how insights from learning sciences and human-computer interaction can inform these systems' design and evaluation in-the-wild.

Papers

EduCoder: An Open-Source Annotation System for Education Transcript Data Saad Ashraf, Jim Malamut, Vishal Kumar, Guanzhong Pan, HyunJi Nam, Mei Tan, Lucía Langlois, Liliana Carolina Santos-Deonizio, Helen Spencer Higgins, Dorottya Demszky ACL 2026 System Demonstrations

EduCoder is an open-source web platform for annotating classroom conversation transcripts. It combines transcript text, video, and instructional materials into a single annotation workspace, and supports team-based and human–AI collaborative annotation.

Demo · PDF
DiagramIR: An Automatic Pipeline for Educational Math Diagram Evaluation Vishal Kumar, Shubhra Mishra, Rebecca Hao, Rizwaan Malik, David Broman, Dorottya Demszky NeurIPS Math-AI 2025

DiagramIR introduces a scalable method for automatic evaluation of diagram quality when no ground truth exists. The method achieves higher agreement with human raters and enables smaller models to match frontier ones at a fraction of the inference cost.

GitHub
AmpleGCG-Plus: A Strong Generative Model of Adversarial Suffixes to Jailbreak LLMs with Higher Success Rates in Fewer Attempts * denotes equal contribution. Vishal Kumar*, Zeyi Liao*, Jaylen Jones, Huan Sun Poster at COLM 2024

AmpleGCG-Plus is a suite of LLMs for rapidly "jailbreaking" other LLMs. Based on Ample-GCG, our models learn the GCG jailbreaking algorithm and generalize to any harmful query, enabling scalable automatic red-teaming.

HuggingFace
Somm: A wine recommendation engine Palak Arora, Pavel Kovalev, Vishal Kumar, Veronica Miaatto, Jade Nguyen Erdos Institute Data Science Showcase

Somm helps new wine drinkers navigate the wine market via simple queries about desired flavor notes. We built a retrieval and re-ranking algorithm over expert wine reviews. The project won a top prize among 500+ students at the Erdos Institute.
