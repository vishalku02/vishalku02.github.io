Let's give the sea corals less homework.

Just like you, corals can only handle so much heat before they crack. Every small thing you do to keep CO₂ down keeps the ocean a little cooler — and the reef a little less stressed.

Walk, bike, or carpool

Cars and buses burn gas, which is one of the biggest sources of CO₂. Every car ride you skip is a tiny win for the ocean.

Power down at home

Turn off lights and screens you aren't using. Unplug chargers. A lot of electricity still comes from burning fuels — saving energy = cooler oceans.

Eat what's local

Your community already does this with salmon. Food trucked or flown from far away has a huge carbon footprint — local food doesn't.

Spread the word

Tell your family, your classmates, your community what you learned. Big change starts with lots of small voices saying the same thing.

A reef on the other side of the planet is connected to your dinner plate.
So is everything you do today.
